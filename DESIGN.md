# Design System

## Theme

Light industrial corporate website with a restrained brick-red, charcoal, and white palette. Cool pale-blue surfaces are reserved for low-emphasis placeholders and utility icons, not primary actions.

## Color

- Brand brick red: `#8b3a2f`
- Ink: `#222222`
- Body text: `#444444`
- Muted text: `#797979`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Quiet surface: `#f6f6f6`
- Navigation surface: `#333333`

## Typography

`OPPO Sans 4.0`, with `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks. The official variable font covers weights 100-700; the site uses regular body text and heavier headings with compact line-height. Body copy keeps a 1.7 line-height for Chinese readability.

## Layout

Main content is constrained to 1170px. Sections use a consistent full-width band with responsive one-column fallbacks below 768px.

## Components

Use compact square-corner controls, simple line borders, content-led cards, the persistent contact toolbar, and brick-red active states. When a module's content already begins with a divider, use the flush title variant so no extra vertical gap appears between the title and content.

The homepage About preview is an unframed two-column band: image on the left and linked title/summary on the right. Clamp the summary to six lines on desktop and four on mobile, stack the columns on narrow screens, and keep both areas linked to `/zh/about`.
